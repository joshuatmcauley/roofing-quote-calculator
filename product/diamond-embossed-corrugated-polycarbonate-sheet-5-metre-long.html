About this product

Corolite Diamond is a premium, heavy-duty corrugated polycarbonate roofing sheet designed for strength, durability, and performance. With an increased thickness of 2.8mm, it offers enhanced rigidity, excellent spanning ability, and superior load-bearing capacity. The naturally clear finish delivers impressive, glass-like light transmission, while the embossed diamond pattern adds both strength and visual appeal. Engineered to withstand extreme temperatures, UV exposure, and harsh weather conditions, Corolite Diamond is a reliable solution for long-term applications.
