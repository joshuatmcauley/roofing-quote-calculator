About this product

WPC garden shed model DeLux 9m²

The new 9 m² PREMIUM composite Delux model shed positions itself as one of the most advanced solutions in the current market segment. It combines architectural modernity, structural robustness, and virtually maintenance-free materials, making it an appealing choice for homeowners looking for more than just storage space.

1. Construction & Materials

The structure relies on an aluminum frame—an excellent choice for dimensional stability and long-term resistance to corrosion. Into this frame are fitted 28 mm co-extruded composite slats.
Co-extrusion is a high-end process that encapsulates the composite core with a protective outer layer. This improves:

  • UV resistance, preventing fading and color shifts

  • Waterproofing, limiting swelling or warping common in low-quality composites

  • Resistance to stains and scratches, allowing the shed to retain a “like-new” appearance for years

The composite formulation (60% wood fibers, 30% polymer, 10% additives) is consistent with premium outdoor materials and contributes to a realistic aged-wood appearance with no maintenance constraints.

2. Design & Usability

This model follows the recent architectural trends of the outdoor living sector: 90° lines, contemporary colors, and a flat roof. With internal dimensions of nearly 9 m² (296×296 cm) and a comfortable wall height of 2 m, the shed can genuinely serve as an additional room—be it an office, a workshop, or a high-end storage space.

The 148.5 cm-wide double aluminum doors with 5-6-5 mm tempered double glazing are a notable upgrade over standard PVC doors. They enhance natural light, improve insulation, and make access easier for large tools, bikes, or garden equipment. Ventilation grilles at the back are another functional element often missing in this category.

3. Roofing & Weather Protection

Delux includes a PVC + ASA corrugated waterproof roof, which is a major advantage. Unlike felt or bitumen roofing—common in mid-range sheds—PVC+ASA provides:

  • Very high UV resistance

  • Excellent durability (no periodic replacement)

  • Full waterproofing

  • No maintenance

The ability to add an aluminum gutter and a 75 mm downpipe improves water management and allows for rainwater harvesting—an increasingly demanded feature.

The snow-load capacity of 100 kg/m² is above average for the segment, making it suitable for harsher climates.

4. Installation Requirements

The requirement for a 3630 × 3395 mm concrete slab aligns with the level of quality offered. A stable foundation is essential to maintain precision in door alignment and ensure the longevity of the aluminum/composite structure.

5. Overall Assessment

The Delux 9 m² PREMIUM composite garden shed delivers a level of quality rarely found in mainstream garden structures. The combination of co-extruded composite, aluminum framing, and high-quality glazing positions it closer to a small modern outbuilding than a traditional “shed.”
