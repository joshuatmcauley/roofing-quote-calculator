About this product

The B&C McKeown Skylight 5.95m² garden shed is one of the newest additions to the Woodlife Garden composite range — and it fully embraces modern garden design. With its clean vertical lines, sharp angles, flat roof, and contemporary anthracite finish, this shed blends seamlessly into today’s stylish outdoor spaces.

Designed for Modern Living

This model reflects current architectural trends while delivering the durability and performance Woodlife Garden is known for. Its sleek profile makes it an attractive focal point in any garden, rather than just a storage solution.

Built for Strength and Longevity

At the heart of this shed is a premium co-extruded composite material made from a high-quality blend of resin and wood. The advanced outer layer enhances resistance to moisture, stains, and weathering, ensuring long-lasting performance with minimal maintenance.

Key durability features include:

  • Exceptionally robust composite construction

  • Fully waterproof painted metal roof – no additional roof covering required, saving on overall costs

  • Snow load capacity of 50 kg/m²

Practical and Secure

Functionality is just as impressive as its design. The shed features a high-quality double door (139.8 x 187.5 cm) for easy access, ideal for storing bulky tools, lawn equipment, bikes, or garden furniture. The aluminium doors are lockable for added security and can be installed on the front or side panels (not the rear).

The semi-glazed panels with 2.5 mm acrylic glazing maximise natural light inside, creating a bright and usable interior space — perfect for use as:

  • A secure garden storage solution

  • A DIY or hobby workshop

  • Winter storage for garden furniture

  • Housing for swimming pool equipment
