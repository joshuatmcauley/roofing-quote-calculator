About us

A County Down supplier of composite building products, roofing sheets and landscaping materials.

Who we are

Local supply,
no runaround

We stock the outdoor materials people around here actually ask for: roofing sheets for sheds, barns and lean-tos, composite decking and cladding that does not need painting, fencing that stands up to coastal wind, and granite for paths, steps and kerbs.

Most of what we sell is cut or made up to the size you need rather than sold in fixed packs. That keeps waste down and means a small job is worth doing properly.

If you are not sure what you need, bring your measurements. We would rather spend ten minutes getting the order right than have you back on Saturday for one more sheet.
