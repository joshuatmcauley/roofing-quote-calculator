Cut to size

Pay for the roof you have

Box profile and tile effect come off the roll and get cut to the metre. A 4.3 metre slope is a 4.3 metre sheet, not a 5 metre sheet with 700 mm in the skip.

Insulated panels and polycarbonate come in fixed stock lengths, so there we will tell you which length to take and what the offcut will be before you commit.
